What is the ink absorber?

Inside your Canon MP280 printer are small sponge-like pads called ink absorbers (or waste ink pads).
They collect extra ink that gets flushed out during:

Print-head cleaning

Deep cleaning cycles

Borderless printing

Power on/off maintenance

Over time, those pads soak up a lot of ink.

What does error P07 / 5B01 mean?

The printer has an internal counter that estimates how much ink has gone into those pads.

When it reaches a preset limit:

The printer assumes the pads are full

It locks itself to avoid ink overflowing inside the printer

You see error P07 / 5B01 – Ink absorber is full

This does not mean the printer is broken—it’s a safety shutdown.

Why does Canon say “qualified technician only”?

Because officially:

The printer must be opened

The ink absorber pads replaced or cleaned

The internal counter reset

Canon treats this as a service-center repair, especially for older models like this one.

Important things to know

❌ Replacing ink cartridges will not fix this

❌ Turning the printer off/on will not fix this

✔️ The printer is protecting itself from internal ink spills

✔️ The printer can work again after pad service + counter reset

What are your options?

You have three realistic choices:

Service center repair

Safe but often more expensive than the printer itself

DIY cleaning + counter reset

Many users clean the pads and reset the counter

Works, but involves opening the printer and getting messy

Replace the printer

Common choice due to the age of the MP280
